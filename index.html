02 / THE IDEAS
01

Read the surface.

A scalar field gives each point a value. Here colour shows surface slope, estimated from neighbouring samples.

02

Choose a direction.

A vector field assigns directions. Our blend rotates a simple planar direction: a visual introduction, not a principal-curvature or stacking solver.

03

Make some space.

A normal offset moves points away from a surface. Our example illustrates the idea; it does not check self-intersection or fabrication clearance.
